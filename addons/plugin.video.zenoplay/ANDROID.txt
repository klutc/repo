ZenoPlay on Kodi Android / Android TV

The add-on uses ZenoPlay's AJAX endpoint to retrieve its player list and sends the selected provider URL to the shared Find Stream URL resolvers. The repository includes native handlers for the ZenoPlay provider families VidSrcEmbed, RuPlayer/SPXPlayer and Morencius, plus the general host resolvers.

Playback does not launch Chrome for hosts with native resolver support. Some providers may still require a desktop browser or may reject requests from a particular network; in that case choose another provider. Subtitle providers receive the original show title, IMDb id when available, season and episode through Kodi's VideoInfoTag. Embedded Bulgarian captions are downloaded and converted to Kodi's local subtitle format.

On Android, Upbolt and StreamTape-family providers use the MyVidPlay Bridge WebView helper to complete a visible Cloudflare check and return a direct media URL when the player exposes one. VidSrcEmbed stays on the native resolver first and opens the helper only if it reports a Cloudflare/Turnstile challenge. These fallbacks are experimental and do not support DRM or browser-only `blob:` streams.
